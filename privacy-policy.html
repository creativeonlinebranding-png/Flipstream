Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how FlipStream handles information when you use the application.

Effective Date: 20 September 2026

FlipStream is a music streaming application that allows users to discover and listen to music, explore artist profiles, and access music-related content.

FlipStream respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the FlipStream application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

FlipStream does not currently require users to create an account or log in.

FlipStream does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Passwords
  • Payment information

Users can use the current version of FlipStream without creating an account.

Automatically Collected Information

The current version of FlipStream is designed to operate without requiring users to provide personal information.

However, basic technical information may potentially be processed by the device, operating system, hosting services, or other technical services required to deliver the application. Such information may include technical information necessary for the application to function properly.

2. How Information Is Used

FlipStream may process limited technical information when necessary to:

  • Operate the application
  • Stream music
  • Deliver music and artwork
  • Maintain application functionality
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Improve reliability and performance

FlipStream does not sell personal information.

3. Music and Artist Content

FlipStream contains music, artwork, artist names, biographies, and other music-related content.

Some artist information may be displayed publicly within the application for the purpose of promoting and providing information about the artist and their music.

Artists or rights holders who believe that their content has been displayed without authorization can contact us using the email addresses provided below.

4. Advertising

FlipStream currently does not display advertisements.

No advertising network is currently used by FlipStream.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated where required.

5. Login and Accounts

FlipStream currently does not require user accounts or login.

Users can access the current version of the application without registering an account.

If account functionality is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how account information is handled.

6. Third-Party Services

FlipStream may rely on technical services such as hosting or content-delivery services to make music and other application content available.

Such services may process technical information necessary to deliver their services.

FlipStream does not currently use third-party advertising services.

7. External Links

FlipStream may provide links to external websites, social media profiles, music platforms, or other third-party services.

When you leave FlipStream and visit an external service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

FlipStream is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services.

8. Children's Privacy

FlipStream does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

Because the current version does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information, users can access the application without submitting personal information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with FlipStream and to maintain the security and reliability of the application.

However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Because FlipStream currently does not require user accounts or collect personal information for account creation, there is no user account information that we currently retain for this purpose.

Technical information processed by third-party services may be retained according to those services' own policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FlipStream introduces new features, changes how information is handled, or when legal or regulatory requirements change.

Any updated Privacy Policy will include a revised effective date.
